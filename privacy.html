Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Studio is a private tool that a single creator runs on their own computer to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Telegram accounts and to read the statistics of those accounts.

What data is used

  • Account identifiers and access tokens granted through the official sign-in of each platform.
  • Statistics, comments and metadata of the creator's own videos.

Where it is stored

Only on the creator's computer, in a local database. Tokens are encrypted with the operating system's protection. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

Studio uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google. You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

How long data is kept

Public statistics of channels the creator does not own are refreshed regularly and erased if they have not been refreshed for 30 days. Data of the creator's own channels is kept while the channel stays connected.

Cookies

The app sets one session cookie on the creator's own computer to keep them signed in. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Deleting data

Disconnecting a YouTube channel in the app revokes the Google token and deletes all data received from the API for that channel; disconnecting TikTok or Instagram deletes its tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the security settings of each platform. To request deletion, contact the contact listed on the channel's About page.
